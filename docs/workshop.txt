description=
description=    [b]1. In-Game F10 Control Center (7 Tabs)[/b]
description=    [list]
description=    [*] [b]Tab 1 - Quick Actions & Presets:[/b] One-click automated sweeps, manual area cleanups, launcher RAM boosters, and custom profile
description=  management (Potato Mode, Balanced, Max Quality, and User Custom Presets).
description=    [*] [b]Tab 2 - General & Engine:[/b] Dynamic Multi-Story Building Occlusion Culling, Skeletal Animation LOD blending limits, Zombie 2D
description=  Imposter rendering, and low-spec lighting rates.
description=    [*] [b]Tab 3 - Vehicles:[/b] Anti-stutter high-speed chunk streamer, parked vehicle Bullet physics sleeping, driving priority lighting, and
description=  headlight shadow management.
description=    [*] [b]Tab 4 - Blood & Corpses:[/b] Smart blood decal stacking cap (max 4 per tile to eliminate GPU overdraw while keeping full gore),
description=  automated corpse decay sweeps, and safe base radius exclusion.
description=    [*] [b]Tab 5 - World Debris:[/b] Safe ground item sweeps (removes broken glass, cans, rotten food, and litter while strictly preserving
description=  player bases, safehouses, generators, tools, and backpacks).
description=    [*] [b]Tab 6 - Audio & Climate:[/b] Screen-space dynamic puddle shader toggles, rain splash particle clamps, animal pen audio limits,
description=  burning zombie scream throttles, and fly sound emitter caps.
description=    [*] [b]Tab 7 - Mod Profiler & Shield:[/b] Real-time mod execution monitor, 3rd-party GC freeze interceptor, log I/O spam throttler, and
description=  vehicle mechanics null-recipe crash shields.
description=    [/list]

description=    [b]2. Integrated High-Performance GLSL Shaders[/b]
description=    [list]
description=    [*] [b]Lightweight Screen-Space Puddles:[/b] Replaces heavy multi-octave Perlin noise iterations with a streamlined specular pass, cutting
description=  GPU fillrate load by up to 40% during rain.
description=    [*] [b]Low-Overhead Foliage & Wind Shader:[/b] Simplifies trigonometric vertex calculations for tree wind sway to prevent framerate drops
description=  in dense forests.
description=    [*] [b]Optimized Rain Streaks:[/b] Single-pass alpha blend that renders clean rain cascades without multi-layer particle lag.
description=    [/list]
description=
description=    [b]3. Dynamic Multi-Story Building Occlusion Culling[/b]
description=    [list]
description=    [*] Automatically clamps vertical rendering on upper floors (Z >= 2) to eliminate off-screen floor draw calls.
description=    [*] Event-driven on movement with state-delta caching for zero-stutter transitions and immediate exterior visibility near windows, stairs,
description=  and balconies.
description=    [/list]
description=
description=    [b]4. Combat Burst & Shotgun Impact Smoother[/b]
description=    [list]
description=    [*] Buffers blood splats and multi-kill gore across adjacent frames, eliminating the classic frame-freeze when firing shotguns into large
description=  hordes.
description=    [/list]
description=
description=    [b]5. Parked Vehicle Bullet Physics Sleeping[/b]
description=    [list]
description=    [*] Puts stationary, unoccupied vehicles into low-overhead physics sleep mode, waking them up instantly when approached or entered.
description=    [/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/prop11/PZO-Launcher/Unstable/docs/gifs/04_vehicle_road_streaming.gif[/img]

description=  driving.
description=    [*] [b]Log Disk Throttler:[/b] Throttles runaway informational log spam to prevent disk write stalls while preserving 100% of errors and
